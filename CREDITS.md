## Code we use or port


**In the player** (`engine/player`):
- [SDL3](https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL) for the window, the keyboard (its raw keyboard thread) and the GPU API.
- [vpin](https://github.com/francisdb/vpin) to read the table file, and its glTF export as the backup way to build the table's model (`--shapes export`) — its rules for which pieces a part has and each piece's material and picture ported into our own model of the table (`engine/player/src/model.rs`, MIT, by Francis De Brabandere, notice in the file).
- [image](https://github.com/image-rs/image) (MIT or Apache-2.0) to decode the table's images, with [exr](https://github.com/johannesvollmer/exrs) by Johannes Vollmer (BSD-3-Clause), the OpenEXR reader it uses, called directly to read big light maps straight into half floats.
- [Symphonia](https://github.com/pdeljanov/Symphonia) (MPL-2.0), by Philip Deljanov and the Project Symphonia developers, to decode music and compressed sounds, with its helpers [RustFFT](https://github.com/ejmahler/RustFFT), num-complex, primal-check, strength_reduce, transpose, extended, regex-lite and lazy_static (MIT or Apache-2.0).
- [serde_json](https://github.com/serde-rs/json) (MIT or Apache-2.0) to read the export.
- [flate2](https://github.com/rust-lang/flate2-rs) with [miniz_oxide](https://github.com/Frommi/miniz_oxide) (both MIT or Apache-2.0) to unpack primitives' animation frames.
- [roxmltree](https://github.com/RazrFalcon/roxmltree) by Yevhenii Reizner and [base64](https://github.com/marshallpierce/rust-base64) by Marshall Pierce (both MIT or Apache-2.0) to read backglass files.
- The public-domain 8×8 VGA font by Marcel Sondaar, as included in SDL3, for the on-screen readouts.
- Microsoft's FXC shader compiler (part of Windows).
- **Ported from VPX for it:**
  - the camera (`renderer/ViewSetup.cpp`, its window mode and the settings that override a table's view included)
  - its window settings and how it places and shows its windows (`core/Settings_properties.inl`, `renderer/Window.cpp`, `Renderer::RenderAncillaryWindow`)
  - its game actions and their mappings to any device's buttons, its joysticks' names and default layouts (`input/InputAction.cpp`, `input/ButtonMapping.h`, `input/InputManager.cpp`, `input/SDLInputHandler.h`)
  - its analog plunger's handler, sensor and Kalman filter (`input/PlungerHandler.cpp`, `input/PlungerHandler.h`, `input/PlungerKalmanFilter.h`, `math/ExponentialMovingAverage.h`; the headers GPL by VPX's licence)
  - its gamepad nudge, the nudge intent it reads from a stick, its model of a real cabinet and its tilt bob (`physics/cabinet/GamepadNudge.cpp`, `NudgeIntentHandler.cpp`, `CabinetPhysics.cpp`, `DampedHarmonicOscillator.h`, `PlumbHandler.cpp` and `NudgeHandler.h`, in `engine/physics-compat`)
  - its gamepad rumble (`InputManager::PlayRumble`, `SDLInputHandler`)
  - its physics settings files beside a table (`PinTable::ImportVPP`, in `engine/table-physics`, which reads them with roxmltree too)
  - the bounds it fits the legacy camera to, and the keyboard code table (`input/ScanCodes.h`)
  - the behaviour of VPX's script objects — among them the lights with VPX's bulb model (`bulb.cpp`), the score reels (`dispreel.cpp`), the animations of targets, triggers and bumpers, primitives' animation frames (`primitive.cpp`'s `PlayAnim` to `UpdateAnimation`, `Mesh::UploadToVB`), the sound rules of `PlaySound`, the table's materials as scripts change them (`UpdateMaterial`, `GetMaterial`) and the parts' picture and material setters — the objects' names, arguments and ids generated from VPX's interface definition (`vpinball.idl`) by `tools/idl-gen`
- **Ported from VPX's drawing:**
  - flashers (`flasher.cpp` and its shader `fs_flasher.sc`, with the blends of `common.sh`)
  - light maps and primitives' color and opacity (`primitive.cpp`, `fs_unshaded.sc`, `fs_basic.sc`)
  - the lights on the backdrop and bulb lights' glow (`light.cpp`, `fs_light.sc`)
  - the score display in text boxes and DMD flashers with VPX's legacy DMD shader (`textbox.cpp`, `flasher.cpp`, `fs_dmd.sc`; the frames of `ScriptGlobalTable.cpp`'s `DMDPixels`), and in its own window as VPX's score view plugin places it (`plugins/scoreview/ScoreView.cpp`), with VPX's newer display renderer (`fs_display.sc`, `vs_dmd.sc`, `Renderer::SetupDMDRender` and `SetupDisplayRenderer`) and its display profiles' default settings
  - the backglass as VPX's B2S plugin reads and draws it, with its script server (`plugins/b2s`: `B2SDataModel.cpp`, `B2SRenderer.cpp`, `B2SServer.cpp`, `B2SPlugin.cpp`; `Renderer::DrawImage`)
  - `PolygonToTriangles` (`MeshUtils.h`), how `BlendDisableLighting` blends towards the unlit color (`material.sh`), and the order see-through parts are drawn in (`RenderDevice::DrawMesh`)
  - for VPX's look (`--look vpx`): its tonemapping (`fs_pp_tonemap.sc`, `Renderer::SetupTonemapping`), the single-pass blends of bulb lights and additive flashers (`light.cpp`, `flasher.cpp`), and its basic material lit by the table's environment picture (`fs_basic.sc`, `material.sh`, the environment lookups of `common.sh`, the irradiance pass `fs_pp_irradiance.sc`; `Shader::SetMaterial`, `Renderer::SetupShaders`, and the built-in materials of `light.cpp`, `spinner.cpp` and `bumper.cpp`), with high-range environment pictures kept as half floats by [half](https://github.com/VoidStarKat/half-rs) (MIT or Apache-2.0), by Kathryn Long
  - its normal mapping (`fs_basic.sc`'s `normal_map` and `TBN_trafo`)
  - its playfield reflections (`RenderProbe::RenderReflectionProbe`, `fs_basic.sc`'s `compute_reflection`, `vs_basic.sc`'s clip plane, the probes' depth range in `ViewSetup`, `MatrixPlaneReflection`)
  - its ball (`fs_ball.sc`, `Ball::Render`, `Ball::RenderSetup`) with VPX's default ball picture (`BallEnv.exr`, used unchanged, `engine/player/assets/vpx/ball`)
  - its bloom (`fs_pp_bloom.sc`, `fs_pp_blur.sc`, `Renderer::UpdateBloom`, `RenderDevice::DrawGaussianBlur`), whose soft threshold follows Jasper Flick's [Catlike Coding](https://catlikecoding.com/unity/tutorials/advanced-rendering/bloom/) bloom tutorial (its code MIT-0)
- **The tone mappers** carry the sources VPX names for them: Khronos's PBR Neutral ([KhronosGroup/ToneMapping](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/ToneMapping), Apache-2.0), and AgX as [three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js) (MIT) and [Filament](https://github.com/google/filament) (Apache-2.0) implement it, after Troy Sobotka's AgX and Benjamin Wrensch's minimal version; the Filmic curve is Jim Hejl and Richard Burgess-Dawson's, as John Hable published it.
- **HDR10 output** for screens with HDR on (`render/hdr10.rs`) follows VPX's own code: the PQ curve of SMPTE ST 2084, and a spline VPX's authors based on [VideoLAN](https://www.videolan.org/)'s and [libplacebo](https://code.videolan.org/videolan/libplacebo)'s HDR tone mapping, as VPX notes it.
- **The dither:** VPX's comes from a Shadertoy page whose license we couldn't confirm, so ours is our own noise, made with Chris Wellons's public-domain integer hash `lowbias32` ([hash-prospector](https://github.com/skeeto/hash-prospector), Unlicense).
- **PinUP packs** are read and played as VPX's PinUP Player plugin does, with its script object `PinUpPlayer.PinDisplay` (`plugins/pup`: `PUPManager.cpp`, `PUPScreen.cpp`, `PUPPlaylist.cpp`, `PUPTrigger.cpp`, `PUPCustomPos.cpp`, `PUPMediaManager.cpp`, `PUPLabel.cpp`, `PUPPinDisplay.cpp`, `PUPPlugin.cpp`, `common.cpp`; the plugin is, in its authors' words, "a rewrite of NailBuster's excellent PinUp Player as a plugin, based on Jason Millard port for VPX standalone", from the community's research into the pack format, credited below); its media player and FFmpeg loader carry no GPL mark and aren't ported.
- **The packs' media** are decoded by [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org) (8.0, LGPL-2.1-or-later; built by us from source at VPX 5436's commit and with its configuration, no GPL parts — `tools/thirdparty`, the build calling itself "LGPL version 2.1 or later" — where VPX 5436's own build, loaded before, lists GPL-2.0, GPL-3.0 and LGPL-3.0; with the small libraries it loads from MSYS2's UCRT64 packages, zlib (zlib licence), bzip2 (its BSD-style licence), xz (0BSD and public domain) and libiconv (LGPL-2.1-or-later), and MinGW's runtime (GCC's runtime exception, winpthreads' MIT and ZPL)), which also writes a table's preview video as H.264 through Windows' own Media Foundation encoder (part of Windows, used, not redistributed), through a bridge of ours compiled against its headers (kept unchanged in `vendor/ffmpeg`) and built with the [cc](https://github.com/rust-lang/cc-rs) crate (MIT or Apache-2.0).
- **The packs' labels:** their text is drawn by [SDL_ttf](https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL_ttf) (zlib), as VPX draws it, with [FreeType](https://freetype.org) — portions of this software are copyright © 2026 The FreeType Project (www.freetype.org), all rights reserved, used under the FreeType License — and [HarfBuzz](https://harfbuzz.github.io) (MIT), all built into the player through the [sdl3-ttf-sys](https://codeberg.org/maia/sdl3-sys-rs) crates (zlib) by maia; VPX's font of last resort, Liberation Sans, by Red Hat with digitized data by Google, is built in under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`engine/player/assets/fonts`).
- **FlexDMD:** ours is ported from [FlexDMD](https://github.com/vbousquet/flexdmd) 1.9.1 by Vincent Bousquet (Apache-2.0; its notice and licence in `engine/player/src/flexdmd/LICENSE-FlexDMD.txt`), with the two MIT-licensed pieces it carries: Jacob Albano's [Glide](https://github.com/jacobalbano/glide) tweens and easing curves, and Cyotek's AngelCode bitmap-font reader; its GIFs are decoded by the [gif](https://github.com/image-rs/image-gif) crate (MIT or Apache-2.0), which `image` uses too. FlexDMD's own fonts and pictures are built in unchanged (listed below); its pictures are decoded, as FlexDMD decodes them, by Windows' GDI+ (part of Windows; used, not redistributed). Which members its objects answer and how its displays reach VPX's follow VPX 5436's FlexDMD plugin (`FlexDMDPlugin.cpp`, GPL).
- **Table scripts** run in [libwinevbs](https://github.com/vpinball/libwinevbs) (LGPL-2.1-or-later), loaded as its own DLL, which we build from its source with three patches of ours (`tools/libwinevbs`), with mingw-w64's winpthreads (`libwinpthread-1.dll`, MIT and BSD-3-Clause) beside it. It's built with [MSYS2](https://www.msys2.org/) (BSD-3-Clause) and its UCRT64 toolchain: [GCC](https://gcc.gnu.org/) (GPL-3.0-or-later with the GCC runtime library exception), [GNU Bison](https://www.gnu.org/software/bison/) for the VBScript parser (GPL-3.0-or-later; the parsers it writes carry its exception) and [mingw-w64](https://www.mingw-w64.org/)'s tools, among them Wine's interface compiler `widl`.
- **Random numbers** come from VPX's MWC64X generator (by David Thomas), ported with the physics; build 5436 uses it for everything random.

## Projects we learn from

- **[PPUC Colorizing DMD](https://github.com/PPUC/PPUC-Colorizing-DMD)** — the PPUC project (GPL-3.0-or-later). The frame dump its coloring editor opens, which the player and the app's Color it… write for a machine's score display; the editor itself is its makers', installed on a click from their newest release on GitHub once they publish one, never shipped with ours.
- **[ColorizingDMD](https://github.com/SerumColor/ColorizingDMD)** — zesinger (GPL-2.0), the Windows coloring editor PPUC's is to replace. Its loader read to learn what a frame dump needs for it, a blank line after each frame, which ours write. The app installs it on a click from its newest release on GitHub, never shipping it with ours.
- **[RetroAchievements](https://retroachievements.org)** — the RetroAchievements team. Achievements for emulated retro games, the model for ours; its condition library, [rcheevos](https://github.com/RetroAchievements/rcheevos) (MIT), weighed before ours were written afresh.
- **VPX Achievement Watcher** — Solters ([on VPUniverse](https://vpuniverse.com/forums/topic/14222-vpx-achievement-watcher-add-achievements-stats-challenges-to-vpx/)). Achievements, challenges and a leaderboard for VPX, read from PinMAME's saved memory: prior art for ours.
- **[Pinball Memory Maps](https://github.com/tomlogic/pinball-memory-maps)** (named pinmame-nvram-maps until August 2026) — Tom Collins (tomlogic) and contributors (ODbL for the database, DbCL for its contents). Where ROM games keep their scores and counters in their saved memory: used for ROM tables' high scores (above) — in the app, and in the player for the screenshot when a game makes them — and for the state of a ROM table's game that achievements follow, such as the ball in play and a tilt.

- **[Visual Pinball X](https://github.com/vpinball/vpinball)** — the vpinball team. Table format, script API, physics, plugin architecture. Its headless physics test harness (`tests/physics`, GPL-3.0-or-later) is built locally to produce the reference runs our physics port is checked against, with [doctest](https://github.com/doctest/doctest) (MIT, Viktor Kirilov), VPX's test framework. Its plugins — rebuilds of PinMAME's controller, B2S and PinUP Player, mostly GPL-3.0-or-later — guide our versions of the objects table scripts use. Its player, build 5436, is built locally with a few marked changes of ours to draw reference pictures and, with its PinMAME plugin, to measure the PinMAME round trip against ours.
- **[Visual Pinball Engine (VPE)](https://github.com/freezy/VisualPinball.Engine)** — freezy and the VPE team (GPL-3.0). An earlier rebuild of VPX on Unity; its physics port, threading model and PinMAME timing studied.
- **[vpxtool](https://github.com/francisdb/vpxtool)** — francisdb. `.vpx` extraction and conversion.
- **[DirectOutput Framework (DOF)](https://github.com/DirectOutput/DirectOutput)** — the DOF team, and the community behind the DOF Config Tool.
- **[B2S Backglass Server](https://github.com/vpinball/b2s-backglass)** — the B2S team. The `.directb2s` format. B2S Backglass Designer's help (Stefan Wuehr, "Herweh", and the B2S team) describes how a backglass's animations play; the player's animation player is written from that description, no code used.
- **[DMD Extensions](https://github.com/freezy/dmd-extensions)** — freezy. Its reader for PIN2DMD's locked `.pac` files (GPL-2.0, `VniLoader.cs`) shows the format `vpi-colour` reads, decompressing with [flate2](https://github.com/rust-lang/flate2-rs) (MIT or Apache-2.0) and decrypting with Windows' own CNG. The user's own copy of it can drive their real DMD panel: `vpi-dmddevice` loads its `DmdDevice64.dll` and hands it frames as VPinMAME does; nothing of it is shipped or ported.
- **[DOF Configtool Client](https://github.com/mkalkbrenner/dof_configtool_client)** — mkalkbrenner (GPL-3.0). Its idea, the user's own tweaks kept as rules re-applied to every fresh download of their DOF Config Tool files, is the plan for our DOF configurator.
- **[FlexDMD](https://github.com/vbousquet/flexdmd)** — Vincent Bousquet (vbousquet). Ported into the player (above); the version installed with VPX is also what our port is checked against.
- **PinUP Player and PinUP Popper** — NailBuster. PUP packs and frontend conventions. VPX's PinUP plugin, which ours is ported from, matches PinUP Player's own behaviour where NailBuster allowed it to be studied (VPX's fixes of October 2026: volumes and ducking, triggers, play actions, screen modes).
- **[PUP pack research](https://gist.github.com/jsm174/e5aa4ebe70052b5cf2ef49ab40c35dfb)** — jsm174 and francisdb, documenting PUP formats. Jason Millard (jsm174)'s port of PinUP Player for VPX standalone is what VPX's PinUP plugin, which ours is ported from, is based on.
- **[PinballY](https://github.com/mjrgh/PinballY)** — Michael J Roberts (mjrgh) (GPL-3.0). Where it and PinballX keep a table's media and how they name them — each screen's folder, a game's description as the name, instruction cards numbered — followed by the table library's media for those front ends (`engine/library/src/frontends.rs`); and where they keep a table's rating — PinballX's game lists, and PinballY's stats file over them, a game named there by its title, maker and year as PinballY names it — followed by the library's ratings (`engine/library/src/ratings.rs`); none of its code copied.
- **[VPin Studio](https://github.com/syd711/vpin-studio)** — syd711 and contributors (MIT). Table, media and high-score management; its multi-source screen view. Studied feature by feature. Ported into the table library (`engine/library`; its MIT notice in `engine/library/LICENSE-VPinStudio.txt`): its matching of a table to VPS — the file-name splitter, the game and table-file matchers, the looser fallback, with the cosine and Jaccard distances it takes from Apache Commons Text — and its rules for telling a drop's files apart (`UploaderAnalysis`).
- **[Virtual Pinball Spreadsheet](https://virtualpinballspreadsheet.github.io)** — the VPS team. Identifies tables and their versions for the table library, fetched from where VPS hosts it into the user's data folder. Its database states no licence, so none of it is kept in our repository.
- **[JojoDiff](https://sourceforge.net/projects/jojodiff/)** — Joris Heirbaut (GPL-3.0-or-later). The binary patch format of VPUniverse's table patches (VPURemix), which VPin Studio applies with JojoDiff's `jptch`; ours applies them itself, through a port of JANPatch's reader of the format.
- **[JANPatch](https://github.com/janjongboom/janpatch)** — Jan Jongboom, Copyright 2017 ARM Ltd. (Apache-2.0). Its reader of JojoDiff's patches (`janpatch.h`) ported to Rust for applying VPU's table patches (`engine/library/src/patch.rs`, its notice in `engine/library/LICENSE-JANPatch.txt`); checked against its demo's JojoDiff patches, both ways, byte for byte.
- **[Pinscape](https://github.com/mjrgh/PinscapePico)** — mjrgh. Cabinet input and output hardware. His [Pinscape Build Guide](http://mjrnet.org/pinscape/BuildGuideV2/BuildGuide.php?sid=toc) and the [Open Pinball Device](http://mjrnet.org/pinscape/OpenPinballDevice/OpenPinballDeviceHID.htm) standard are the main sources of what the player knows of cabinet boards.
- **[Cleveland Software Design](https://github.com/philipellisis/csd-docs)** (CSD) and **[L'atelier d'Arnoz](https://shop.arnoz.com/laboratoire/)** — their documentation of their cabinet boards, read for the same. No code used.
- **Baller Installer** — the bundled installer for today's stack.
- **[oss-virtual-pinball](https://github.com/francisdb/oss-virtual-pinball)** — francisdb's index of open-source virtual pinball projects.
- **[Batocera](https://wiki.batocera.org/systems:vpinball)** — documentation of VPX standalone configuration.
