# Pictures and sounds of our own

Made by Visual Pinball Infinite for the player, which builds them into itself. GPL-3.0-or-later, as the player is, but for the room picture (CC0, below).

- `kickers/` — a picture for each kind of kicker VPX draws with a built-in one (`Kicker::RenderSetup`): `cup.png`, `williams.png`, `gottlieb.png`, `cup2.png` (VPX's "cup 2", its `KickerT1`) and `hole.png` (VPX's wooden hole). VPX's own were drawn in 2014–2017, before its GPL rule, so they can't be taken: these are ours. `tools/kicker-pictures` paints them from the kickers' meshes alone — turned metal for the cups and kickers, plywood for the hole — each tinted to the average colour VPX's picture shows on its kicker, so tables keep the colours their authors saw. Re-run it (`cargo run --release -p kicker-pictures`) after changing how they're painted.
- `room/rostock-laage-airport.png` — the room a table is drawn in, and its parts reflect, when it names no room picture of its own or one it doesn't hold, in place of VPX's built-in airport hall (`EnvMap.webp`, from 2014, before VPX's GPL rule: it can't be taken). Made from Poly Haven's [Rostock-Laage Airport](https://polyhaven.com/a/rostock_laage_airport) by Greg Zaal, **CC0** — free for any use, credit appreciated — by `tools/room-picture` (its README has the source file and how it's graded to VPX's light).
- `sounds/achievement.wav` — the chime played as an achievement is announced, unless its pack brings a sound of its own: two bell-like notes, E6 then B6, made from sine waves by a few lines of our own (0.9 s, 48 kHz), nothing recorded or taken.
