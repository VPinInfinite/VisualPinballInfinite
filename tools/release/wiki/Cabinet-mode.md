# Cabinet mode

Cabinet mode turns the app full screen across a cabinet's screens, for choosing and starting tables with the cabinet's buttons. Open it with **Cabinet mode** at the top of the app, or **Open cabinet mode** in Settings › Cabinet. Opened from a table's page, it starts on that table.

## Its screens

- **The playfield screen** shows **A wheel of their logos** over the chosen table's playfield, or **A row of their playfields** (Settings › Cabinet › **The playfield screen shows**). **Its playfield** chooses **Videos, with their sound**, or **Pictures**. A table without a video shows its picture.
- **The backglass, score display and topper screens** show the chosen table's media where Settings › Screens puts VPX's windows, or where a preset of its own puts them (**Its screens**). The tables it starts play with that preset too, in VPX 10.9 (or 10.8.1), while the desktop app keeps your usual screens.

## The buttons

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| **Flippers** (or ← →) | the previous and next table |
| **Magna-saves** (or ↑ ↓) | the next letter |
| **Start** (or Enter) | play the table |
| **Exit** (or Esc) | the menu |

The buttons are the ones Settings › Controls gives VPX's actions, so a controller works too, its **Back** opening the menu. The mouse works as well: click a table to choose it, the mouse wheel browses, and **Menu** in a corner opens the menu.

## The menu

For a table with more than one build, a **Play …** row for each, to choose which plays; then **Favourites**, **Recently played**, your playlists, **All tables**, **Volume**, **Back to the desktop app**, **Close the app**, and **Shut down the PC** (press Start again to confirm).

## Playing from it

**Start** plays the chosen table in VPX, as PLAY does, and shows "Starting …" until VPX's window is up; cabinet mode waits behind it. A Future Pinball table plays in Future Pinball, as its PLAY does. The pause menu works as it does from the desktop app (see [Playing](Playing#the-pause-menu)). After the game, you're back on the same table.

## When nobody plays

**Through the tables by itself:** after a minute, 3 minutes (the usual), 5 or 10, it moves through the tables on its own until someone presses a button. Or never.

## Its sound

One sound plays at a time: the first of the chosen table's backglass video, topper video and playfield video that has sound, on a screen your cabinet has. **Its sound** in Settings › Cabinet sets their order (the backglass's first, then the topper's, then the playfield's, unless you move them). When none has sound, its music plays: PinUP Popper's audio for it, the music its script plays, its AltSound pack's or PinUP pack's music, or the sound in the preview video the app made for it. **Its volume** sets one level for every table, also in cabinet mode's menu, and the preview videos the app makes are recorded equally loud.

## Its picture

**Its picture** turns cabinet mode's picture for a playfield screen lying another way than Windows has it: **As Windows has it**, **A quarter turn right**, **A quarter turn left** or **Upside down**. PinUP Popper's sideways playfield media show upright either way.

## Starting straight into it

**The app opens in cabinet mode** and **The app starts with Windows**, for a cabinet that starts straight into it.
