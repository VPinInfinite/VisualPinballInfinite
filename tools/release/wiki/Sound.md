# Sound

Settings › Sound. A table's sounds, its music and the machine's own sound, through one set of speakers or two. **-** and **=** change both volumes while you play.

## Speakers

Two outputs, as in VPX:

- **The playfield's sounds:** the ball, the flippers, bumpers and the rest of the table.
- **The backglass's sounds:** music, the machine's sound boards, PinUP packs, and music played through Media Player.

A cabinet often sends the playfield's sounds to the speakers or shakers in its body and the backglass's to the speakers in its backbox. One set of speakers can play both.

## The playfield's speaker layout

- **Stereo:** two speakers, a desk's, a TV's, or a cabinet's own pair. The table's sounds move left and right.
- **Stereo on the rear outputs.**
- **Surround, the rear speakers by the lockbar:** a 5.1 or 7.1 set with the front pair at the table's far end and the rear pair by you, as in a home cinema: the ball rolling toward you comes from behind.
- **Surround, the front speakers by the lockbar:** the same, plugged in the other way round.
- **Cabinet surround (SSF), VPX's older mixing,** and **VPX's newer mixing:** for cabinets with exciters (bass shakers) under the playfield. The newer is what most surround-sound cabinets are set up for.

## Volumes

The playfield's and the backglass's volumes, kept for every table. In the pause menu, a level for each kind of sound a table plays. A machine's own volume is on Settings › Machines.

## Sound packs

**Play a game's AltSound pack when it has one.** AltSound packs replace a machine's music and sounds with remastered ones; turned off, the machine's own sound plays. For one game, its table's settings choose. A machine without a pack can have one made from its ROM, on its table's page. See [AltSound](AltSound).

## Achievements

Each achievement is announced with a sound: its pack's own, or the player's chime. **Play a sound as each achievement is announced** turns it off or on, and its volume goes from 0 to 200%, on top of the backglass volume (100% plays it as it comes). The pause menu changes it too while you play. See [Achievements](Achievements#its-sound).

## A table's music

A table that plays music files finds them in VPX's music folder, as in VPX: the drop window puts them there. Tables that play their music through Windows Media Player play it without it.
