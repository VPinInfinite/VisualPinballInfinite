# Starting tables from a front end

PinUP Popper, PinballX and PinballY start VPX with a table as they always have: there's nothing to change in them. The app works beside them, on the same tables and folders.

## A game started from a front end

- **It plays in VPX as VPX plays it,** with VPX's own menu on F12.
- **It plays with your VPX settings,** the ones the app's settings pages change: your screens, sound, controls and picture. (VPX 10.8.0 keeps settings of its own.)
- **The high scores it makes show on the table's page** all the same, as the app reads them where VPX keeps them.
- **What it doesn't get, as these come with PLAY in the app:** achievements followed as you play, the app's [pause menu](Playing#the-pause-menu), screenshots, Play Hardcore and Play Softcore, a screen preset (a table's own, or cabinet mode's), your plays counted, and the VPX the app chooses for each table. A front end starts the VPX program its own settings name.
- **A library kept in a folder for each game** needs VPX 10.8.1: VPX 10.8.0, and a front end starting tables through it, can't start ROM tables kept that way.

## PinUP Popper

The app works with Popper's folders and game list:

- It shows Popper's media for each game, and can put the ones you choose in Popper's folders (see [Media](Media#media-for-pinup-popper)).
- It shows the ratings you gave tables in Popper (VPin Studio keeps its ratings there too), and can put the ones you give in the app in Popper's list (see [Your library](Your-library#ratings)).
- A table renamed to VPS's name for all players changes Popper's game list with it, the list kept as a copy first.
- A table removed from the library comes off Popper's list, the list kept as a copy first.

## PinballX and PinballY

The media a table uses can go in their folders too: see [Media](Media#media-for-pinballx-and-pinbally). Ratings work as with Popper: theirs show in the app, and yours can go in their lists. A table renamed for all players, or removed from the library, changes their game lists too, each list kept as a copy first.

## Without a front end

The app's own [cabinet mode](Cabinet-mode) chooses and starts tables with a cabinet's buttons, on all its screens.
