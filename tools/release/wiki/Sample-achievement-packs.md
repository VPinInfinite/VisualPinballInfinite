# Sample achievement packs

Ten achievement packs for ten well-known tables, made as examples to play and to learn from. Claude wrote them: their achievements, pictures and sounds. Each is on VPIDB (vpidb.org). To add one, open its table's page in the app: under **More on VPS** › **Achievement packs from VPIDB**, **Add** downloads it and adds it. A pack's zip dropped onto the app adds it too. Each is made for one table file, because the parts it counts are named as that table file names them.

How each achievement is built, and every kind of condition a pack can use: [Sample achievement packs](https://visualpinballinfinite.org/resources/achievement-packs) on the website. How to make your own: [Achievements](Achievements#making-a-pack).

### Attack from Mars

For [Attack from Mars](https://virtualpinballspreadsheet.github.io/?game=9Paf7-CL) (Bally, 1995): the table file by JPSalas, Arngrim, Rothbauerw, JLouLoulou, Kiwi, version 6.0.0. Its sound as each is announced: a flying saucer's rising whirl.

- **The Invasion Begins** (1 point): Start a game.
- **Pop Goes the Martian** (5 points): Hit the pop bumpers 25 times in one ball.
- **Bumper Flurry** (10 points): Hit the pop bumpers 5 times within 3 seconds.
- **Shields Down** (5 points): Knock down the saucer's shield target 3 times in one game.
- **Shake the Saucer** (10 points): Make the saucer shake 5 times in one game (its shaker coil, 15).
- **Double Trouble** (10 points): Have two balls in play at once.
- **First Contact** (5 points): Score 100,000,000 in a game.
- **Martian Billionaire** (25 points): Score 1,000,000,000 in a game. Its own sound: a brass fanfare.
- **The Knock of Victory** (25 points, secret): Win a replay or special: the knocker fires (coil 7).
- **Slingshot Veteran** (15 points): Fire the slingshots 500 times over all your games.
