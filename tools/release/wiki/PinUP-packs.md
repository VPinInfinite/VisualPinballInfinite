# PinUP packs

A PinUP pack is a set of videos, pictures and sounds made for one table. It plays on your other screens (the topper, the backglass, the score display, and sometimes the playfield) while the table plays, started by what happens in the game. Visual Pinball Infinite plays packs itself: PinUP Player isn't needed.

## Getting one in

Drop the pack's download onto the app, with its table or onto its page. It goes into PinUP's `PUPVideos` folder under the name the table's script asks for, which isn't always the pack's own. A pack that's there already can be replaced, or added to with **Append**, for packs that come in parts.

## Choosing its layout

Packs come with a few layouts for different setups (two screens, three with a full DMD, four with a topper), as option files their authors made. Until one is chosen, many packs show nothing.

1. **Layout…** on the table's page, or on the pack's row in Tools › PinUP packs, lists its layouts by their authors' names, with the one **In place** marked.
2. **Try it** starts the table with that layout on your screens, without changing the pack. It isn't counted as a play.
3. Choose one, and the app makes the copies its option files describe, inside the pack's folder. It never runs the files themselves; anything an option file asks for beyond copying inside the pack is shown, for you to do by hand if you want it. A file of yours it replaces goes to the Recycle Bin.

## Every pack: Tools › PinUP packs

The list shows every pack, with the table that plays it, its layout and its **Status**:

- **Complete**
- **No layout chosen:** choose one with **Layout…**
- **Files missing:** it may be an add-on for another pack.
- **Empty folder**
- **Still zipped:** **Unpack…** opens it in the drop window.
- **A folder too deep:** its files are packed one folder down.
- **Set by its script:** the table's script chooses what plays.

**Which packs show** picks all of them, the used or the unused. **Files** opens a pack's folder, **VPS** opens its page on VPS, and the bin moves a pack to the Recycle Bin, after asking.

**No table here plays these:** packs whose table isn't here, or whose table asks for another name. Where a table of yours asks for a name like the pack's, or VPS has a game by its name, a **Possible table match** is named, and **Rename it** makes the pack that table's. A pack made for another version may not fit, so a renamed pack says "renamed from …", with **Undo**.

## On or off for one table

The **Enable** switch on the pack's row, on the table's page, turns it off for that table alone. It's kept in the table's own settings. Settings › PinUP packs › **Playing them** turns every pack off.

## Where it draws

Where a table has both a PinUP pack and a backglass file, Settings › PinUP packs chooses which shows on that window: the pack or the backglass file. **Room around the packs' screens** draws a pack's screens this many pixels in from each side of their window.
