# Achievements

Goals to earn as you play a table: a score, a multiball, an extra ball, the machine's own lamps, switches and coils, or the table's parts hit so many times, even one after another against the clock. They come in **achievement packs**, made by a table's author or anyone else, you included (see [Making a pack](#making-a-pack)), and a table can have several. Ten [sample packs](Sample-achievement-packs) show what a pack can do.

## Adding a pack

- **From VPIDB:** a table's page lists the packs VPIDB (vpidb.org) has for its game, under **More on VPS** › **Achievement packs from VPIDB**. **Add** downloads one, checks it's the file VPIDB lists, and adds it; **Update** brings a newer version. No account is needed.
- **A pack you have:** drop it onto the app like any other download: its zip, its folder, or its `achievements.json` with its pictures. It's added at once, and a notice says so. **Add a pack…** on a table's page, or under **Player hub** › **Achievements**, chooses one with Windows' file picker instead.

A pack names the game it's for, so it goes with every version of that table you have. A pack made for one table file, its parts named as that file names them, goes with that file alone.

## On a table's page

The **Achievements** card, beside **High scores**, shows the achievements of the table's pack: those you've earned lit up, with the day you earned each, and the others dimmed, as a grid of pictures or a list. A secret one reads **Secret achievement** until it's earned. Above them are how many you've earned and their points, and the pack's name, version and makers. Where a table has several packs, choose which one shows from the list.

- **Its kind,** as VPIDB tags it: **Official** (from the table's own author), **Community pack**, **Sample pack**, or **Mod** (built on someone else's pack). **Only on this PC** means VPIDB doesn't have it, so what you earn with it stays here.
- **The sync mark** shows that what you earn with it is shared with VPIDB. Greyed, the pack is on VPIDB but sharing is off.

The trophy button beside PLAY jumps to the card. **What it holds** lists each pack too, with how many you've earned of it.

## Deleting a pack

The bin on a pack's row in **What it holds**, or on its row in **Player hub** › **Achievements**, deletes it after asking. It's deleted for good: it doesn't go to the Recycle Bin, and it goes from every table it's made for. What you've earned with it stays earned. Where VPIDB doesn't have the pack, the question says that this may be the only copy.

## While you play

Each achievement is announced as it's earned: a card on the backglass screen for a few seconds, with a sound, or a note on the playfield where there's no backglass window. It's kept at once, even if the game ends early. As play begins, a card says how this game counts: **Hardcore**, with how many there are to earn, or **Softcore**, with why.

The pause menu's **Achievements** row shows how many you've earned (**3 of 8**, say). Its page lists each one with how far you've got (**12 of 25**), **Earned** or **Not yet**; a secret one reads **A secret achievement** until it's earned. A line there says how this game counts: **Hardcore: the table plays as its author made it**, **Softcore: kept for you, never counted for masteries**, or **Trying the pack: nothing is kept**.

## Its sound

Each achievement is announced with its pack's own sound, or the player's chime where the pack has none. Settings › Sound › **Achievements** turns the sound off (**Play a sound as each achievement is announced**) or sets its volume, on top of the backglass volume. The pause menu's **Sound** has a level for it too, while a table with achievements plays.

## Hardcore and softcore

An achievement counts as **Hardcore** while the table plays as its author made it: its own script, physics, options and difficulty, with no cheats. Played any other way, it's earned as **Softcore**: still shown and kept for you, but never counted towards a mastery. A game is softcore when:

- its physics are changed: a physics file of your own beside it, or a VPX physics set you've changed;
- its script is edited;
- its options or its difficulty are changed in its table settings;
- PinMAME's cheat mode is on for its game.

A table's achievements say when they're softcore there, and why. PLAY says which way it plays (point at it to see), and its **▾** plays one game either way on purpose:

- **Play Hardcore:** as its author made it, your table settings, script edits and physics changes left out for this game. Your screens, sound and controls stay yours.
- **Play Softcore:** its achievements kept as softcore this game: for a guest, say.

**Player hub** › **Achievements** counts what you've earned each way: click **Hardcore** or **Softcore** to count only those.

## Masteries

Earning every achievement of a pack in Hardcore masters it. When a pack's author updates it, what you earned with the earlier version stays earned, and a mastery keeps the version it was made with.

## Sharing on VPIDB

VPIDB (vpidb.org) is a free home for virtual pinball, separate from Visual Pinball Infinite but from the same maker. **VPIDB** at the top of the app, beside **Add tables**, opens the part of **Settings** › **General** that connects the app to it:

1. Choose what's shared: **Share what I earn**, and **Share my ratings and which tables I have**. Both are ticked to start with.
2. **Connect with Discord…** shows a code and opens VPIDB's page.
3. On VPIDB, sign in with Discord and enter the code. The app says when it's connected, and what it shares.

Once it's connected, either can be switched off at any time:

- **Share what I earn on VPIDB:** what you earn goes to VPIDB as you play, what you'd earned before included. Only Hardcore counts there, for masteries and the lists of who mastered what; softcore shows only on your own page, marked softcore. Off, nothing you earn leaves this PC.
- **Share my ratings and which tables I have:** only which table and version you have, and its stars, go to VPIDB: no file names, folders or play times. VPIDB counts them with everyone's, how many people have each table and its average rating, and shows only the totals. Off, your library and ratings stay on this PC, and VPIDB takes off what it had.

While it's connected, the **VPIDB** button at the top shows a green check. A red ✕ means VPIDB isn't answering: the app tries again every two minutes, and the button says why when you point at it.

**Disconnect** disconnects this PC, here and on VPIDB: what you earned stays on VPIDB, and your tables and ratings are taken off. If VPIDB can't be reached, it disconnects here only, and your page on VPIDB disconnects the PC there.

A pack VPIDB doesn't have, such as one you made yourself, is never shared: what you earn with it stays on this PC.

## Which parts can be counted

The table inspector (**Table editor** at the top, or a table's **…** menu › **Open in the table inspector**) shows a table from above, drawn from its own parts. Click a part to see its name and what an achievement can count of it: each hit, a spinner's spins, a slingshot's kicks. A part the table has its hits turned off for is watched by the player itself when a pack names it, so the table plays exactly as its author made it. A part the ball passes straight through, such as a decoration, can't be counted, and nor can a spinner held between two angles, such as a flap on a ramp.

## Making a pack

**Table editor** at the top: open a table, then **Its achievements**, beside **Its parts**. Across the top are the table's name, **Another table**, and three buttons:

- **Save** keeps what you've made as a draft, with no version, as often as you like. A draft stays until you finish the pack or throw it away, even when the app closes.
- **Try it** plays the table with that pack alone, as softcore. Nothing earned in a try is kept, and afterwards the editor says what you earned.
- **Finalize and pack as 1.1** (or whichever version comes next) makes the pack a version others can play with. What was earned with the version before stays earned, and its masteries keep their version.

**The pack**, at the top right, shows its picture, name, version, achievements and points. Click it to open its details:

- **Pack:** which pack to work on: one of the table's, or **A new pack of my own**, which starts from VPS's facts for the table.
- Its picture (click it to choose one), **Its name**, **Made by** and **What it's about**.
- **Its sound when one's earned:** **Choose…** a sound of your own, or **Use the chime**, the player's.
- **Only for this table file:** tick it when its parts are named as this table names them, so it goes with this table file alone.
- **Show its files**, **Throw the draft away…**, and **Done**.

What's missing before it can be tried or packed is listed there under **Incomplete**, and a warning mark shows on the pack until it's done.

Below, in three columns:

- **The table's parts, on the left,** by kind, with **Find a part by name**. Right-click a part to **Hide it** on the playfield, to reach the parts under it; the eye beside a hidden one shows it again.
- **The playfield, in the middle,** seen from above: **Its look**, as the player draws the table from straight above (**Draw its look** makes that picture the first time), or its parts' **Outlines** alone. Click a part to see what achievements can count of it, then **Count it in this achievement**; right-click one to hide it.
- **The achievements, on the right.** Click one to open it, or **New achievement** to start one: its picture (click it to choose one), its name, what to do in a few words, its points (1 to 100), and **Secret**, its name hidden until it's earned.

Under **Earned when** come its conditions, from **+ Add a condition…**. Only what the table can tell is offered; the others are greyed, with why:

- **A part on the playfield:** its parts, what they do (**is hit**, **spins**, **kicks the ball** for a slingshot, **is rolled over**, **is entered**, **is passed through**) and how many times: **in one ball**, **on ball …** (one ball by its number), **in one game**, **over all your games** or **within some seconds**. Drag parts onto it from the list or the playfield, or use **Pick object**: click the parts to count (those achievements can count stay bright, and a second click takes one out), then **Done**.
- **The score**; **a game starting or ending, or a ball begun**; **balls in play at once**, for a multiball; **an extra ball earned**; **credits on the machine**; or **a tilt**.
- On a ROM table, **a lamp**, **a switch** or **a coil of the machine**, by its number as the machine's manual and PinMAME number it. The box suggests the numbers the table's script uses, its coils by the names the script gives them.
- **An event the table's script tells,** for tables written for achievements.
- **A group** of conditions of its own, to put one inside another.

With more than one condition, choose how they go together:

- **One after another:** with no time limit, or **in one ball**, **on ball …**, **in one game** or **within some seconds**. Tick **In exactly this order** and anything else on the list happening out of turn starts it over.
- **All of these, in any order:** within one ball, one game or some seconds.
- **All at the same moment**, or **Any one of these**.

**Optional: start it over when something happens…** adds what sets it back to nothing, such as a ball going down an outlane: **It starts over each time this happens**, a count over all your games included. Its **×** takes it out again.

**As the pack file has it** shows the achievement as the file keeps it, and **Take it out of the pack** removes it, after asking.
