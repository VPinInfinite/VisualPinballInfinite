# Playing

Press **PLAY** on a table's page, or Start in cabinet mode. The table opens on your screens, with its backglass, score display and PinUP pack where Settings › Screens puts them, and the app steps aside until you're done. For a table with achievements, PLAY's **▾** plays it **Hardcore** or **Softcore** on purpose (see [Achievements](Achievements#hardcore-and-softcore)).

## The keys

VPX's own keys, so they work as they do in VPX. The ones you need most:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| Left and right **Shift** | the flippers (and staged flippers, on tables that have them) |
| Left and right **Ctrl** | the magna-saves |
| **Enter** | the plunger: hold to pull, let go to launch |
| **5** | add a coin |
| **1** | start a game |
| **Z**, **/**, **Space** | nudge from the left, from the right, from the front |
| **P** | the pause menu |
| **Esc** or **Q** | the pause menu, with Quit the table picked |
| **-** and **=** | volume down and up |
| **F12** | screenshot |

Every default key and button, the coin door's and the gamepad's among them, is on [Controls](Controls). Settings › Controls changes any of them, and puts actions on any controller's buttons.

## A gamepad

A gamepad works as soon as it's connected, in VPX's layout for gamepads: the triggers flip, the shoulder buttons are the magna-saves, **A** launches the ball, **B** starts a game, **Y** adds a coin, and the d-pad is the coin door's service buttons. The left stick nudges and the right stick pulls the plunger. **Back** opens the pause menu, and it rumbles where VPX's gamepads rumble.

## Nudging and tilt

The nudge keys and a gamepad's left stick push the table as VPX does, and a cabinet's nudge sensor feels the real cabinet move (see [Controls](Controls)). Nudge too hard and the simulated tilt bob swings far enough to tilt the game, as on a real machine.

## The pause menu

**P**, or a gamepad's **Back**, pauses the game and opens the menu. **Esc** opens it too, with **Quit the table** picked: Esc then Enter quits, and Esc again plays on. Settings › General › **Esc while playing** can make Esc quit at once instead. The mouse works in the menu too.

- **Resume**
- **Sound:** a level for each kind of sound the table plays: the playfield, the backglass, its music, the machine (or its AltSound pack), its PinUP pack, Media Player's music, and the sound its achievements are announced with.
- **Table options:** the options its author built into its script (colors, difficulty, decorations…), handed to the table as you change them.
- **Its view:** the camera, the table redrawn as you change it: view mode, field of view, height, distance, where it looks, size, turn, tilt and more. **Back to its own view** undoes it.
- **Rules:** the table's rules and rule cards.
- **Achievements:** for a table with achievements, how many you've earned, and how far you've got with each (see [Achievements](Achievements#while-you-play)).
- **Controls:** what each key and button does now, the table's own extras among them.
- **Record the display for coloring:** on a ROM machine with a dot-matrix display, records what the display shows from then on, for coloring it (see [Score displays](Score-displays#coloring-your-own)). The row then reads **Recording the display: stop**, with how many frames it has.
- **Quit the table**

What you change is kept for that table when the menu closes. There's nothing to save.

## Volume and screenshots

- **-** and **=** turn both volumes down and up a step at a time (held, they keep going), with a notice on screen. The pause menu has a level for each kind of sound.
- **F12** saves each window you chose in Settings › General › Screenshots, into the data folder's `Screenshots`, by table. A table can also take one by itself when a game makes the high scores (its settings, or Settings › General). They show on its page.

## While it plays

- **Saved values:** what a table's script saves between plays (its own high scores, its options) is kept as VPX keeps it.
- **A machine's first start:** a ROM machine with no memory yet (none here and none from VPX) restores its factory settings first. The player gives it about 15 quiet seconds, so the game then starts as it should.
- **The readout:** frame rate and timings on screen, shown or hidden in Settings › General.
- **Quitting** takes you back to the app, on the same table.
