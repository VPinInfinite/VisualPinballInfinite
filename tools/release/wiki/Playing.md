# Playing

Press **PLAY** on a table's page, or Start in [cabinet mode](Cabinet-mode). The app starts VPX with the table, as a front end does: its backglass, score display and PinUP pack show where Settings › Screens puts them, and the app steps aside once VPX's window is up (cabinet mode stays behind it). Quit the game and VPX closes; the app comes back on the same table.

For a table with achievements, PLAY's **▾** offers two more ways to play it (see [Achievements](Achievements#hardcore-and-softcore)):

- **Play Hardcore:** as its author made it. Your table settings, script edits and physics changes are left out for that game; your screens, sound and controls stay yours.
- **Play Softcore:** its achievements kept as softcore that game, for a guest, say.

With VPX 10.8.1 in your library, a ROM machine VPX has never run starts once first, out of sight, so it sets itself up before your game: the app says it's starting the machine for the first time, and the game follows a few seconds later.

## Which VPX plays it

- **The newest VPX in your library** plays your tables: VPX 10.8.1 where it's there. Where VPX 10.8.0 is there beside it, Settings › Library chooses which one plays, and **How they differ** there compares the two before you switch.
- **One table on its own:** its page's **…** menu › **Play it with VPX 10.8.0**. Its page then says it plays with VPX 10.8.0 for that table alone, and **Play it with Settings › Library's VPX** puts it back.
- **A table that stops at start in 10.8.1:** some older tables' scripts do something VPX 10.8.1 no longer allows, and stop with an error as the table starts. After the game, its page says so and offers **Play it with VPX 10.8.0**, where 10.8.0 is in your library (see [After a game](#after-a-game)). Test it offers it too.
- **ROM tables in VPX 10.8.0** play with the VPinMAME on your PC, not VPX 10.8.1's own PinMAME. Some newer tables need a newer VPinMAME than 10.8.0 came with: with it, their lights, ball and flippers stay off (Mystery Castle v2.4's). Play those in VPX 10.8.1.
- **Tried again by itself:** each time a newer VPX 10.8.1 build is in your library, the app tries the tables you set to play with 10.8.0 in it, out of sight and silent, one at a time and never while you play. Where one plays now, its page says so: **Play it with VPX 10.8.1**, or **Keep VPX 10.8.0**.

In VPX 10.8.0, achievements count once the game is over, not as you play, and the app's pause menu and screenshots aren't there: VPX 10.8.0 takes no add-ons. It also keeps its own settings, which you change in its own Preferences.

## The keys

The main keys, VPX 10.8.1's own until you change them:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| Left and right **Shift** | the flippers (and the upper flippers, on tables that have them) |
| Left and right **Ctrl** | the magna-saves |
| **Enter** | the plunger: hold to pull, let go to launch |
| **5** | add a coin |
| **1** | start a game |
| **Z**, **/**, **Space** | nudge from the left, from the right, forward |
| **F12** | the pause menu |
| **P** | pause; P again plays on |
| **Esc** | quit the game, back to the app |
| **-** and **=** | volume down and up |
| **F9** | a screenshot (the app's key) |

Every action's keys and buttons, the coin door's and a gamepad's among them, are on [Controls](Controls). Settings › Controls changes any of them, and VPX plays with what it saves. VPX 10.8.0 keeps its own keys, set in its own Preferences.

## A gamepad

A gamepad plays in VPX's own layout for gamepads: the triggers flip (pressed further, the upper flippers), the shoulder buttons are the magna-saves, **A** launches the ball, **B** starts a game, **Y** adds a coin, **Back** opens the pause menu, and the d-pad is the coin door's service buttons and moves through menus. The left stick nudges and the right stick pulls the plunger. Where a gamepad's buttons aren't set as that layout, VPX may offer to set them so as a table starts.

## Nudging and tilt

The nudge keys and a gamepad's left stick nudge the table as VPX does, and a cabinet's nudge sensor feels the real cabinet move (see [Controls](Controls)). Nudge too hard and VPX's tilt bob swings far enough to tilt the game, as on a real machine.

## The pause menu

**F12** (VPX's menu key), or a gamepad's **Back**, opens the app's own pause menu over VPX's playfield. VPX pauses while it shows, the table darkened behind it.

- **Resume:** plays on.
- **Volume:** VPX's volume, the playfield's and the backglass's together, kept for your next games.
- **A level for each part of the sound** the game plays: **Table sounds**, **Machine** (or **Machine (AltSound)** with an AltSound pack), **Music**, **PinUP pack**, **Media Player**, **Backglass sounds** and **Achievements**, and any other add-on's sound by its name. Each goes from 0 to 100%: 100% is as the table came, less turns that part down. They're kept for that table. **Put the parts back as they came** sets them all to 100% again.
- **Rules:** the table's instruction cards and flyers, the rules kept in its table file with their pictures, its own pictures that show its rules, and its manuals and read-mes, read in the menu.
- **Guide:** for a table matched to its game on the Virtual Pinball Spreadsheet, where VPS links a guide to the game on Pinball Primer: the guide's own page, as Pinball Primer's site shows it, in a view over the menu. Where VPS links more than one page, a button for each chooses. The magna-saves or ↑ ↓ scroll it, and so do a controller's sticks and d-pad, and the mouse's wheel over it. The page's address, above it, opens it in your browser. It needs the internet, and nothing of it is kept on your PC.
- **Achievements:** for a table with achievements, where they stand: whether the game is hardcore or softcore, how many you've earned, and how far you've got with each (see [Achievements](Achievements#while-you-play)).
- **Controls:** the table's own buttons from its script, then the game's, each on the keys and buttons VPX's settings put it on. Settings › Controls changes them.
- **Its settings:** the table's own settings. VPX reads them as a table starts, so they apply from your next game.
- **VPX's own menu:** for what only VPX changes at once: the view, the table's options, the playfield's and the backglass's volumes apart.
- **Quit the game:** back to the app.

The magna-saves or ↑ ↓ move, the flippers or ← → change a level (the right one also opens a row), and Start or Enter chooses. On a page, a flipper or Esc goes back. Esc on the menu itself plays on, and so does F12. The mouse works too, its right button going back, and so does a controller.

The app's pause menu needs a [newer VPX 10.8.1 build](Installing-and-first-setup#which-vpx-1081-build). In an older one, in VPX 10.8.0, and in a game started from a [front end](Front-ends), VPX's own menu shows instead. To have VPX's own menu in every game, choose **VPX's own** in Settings › Controls › The pause menu.

## After a game

When the game ends, the app reads VPX's log, and where it says something you can act on, the table's page shows it under **From your last game**:

- **VPX ran short of graphics memory:** it skipped some pictures it needed, which show pink or empty. Where supersampling is above 1×, **Set supersampling to 1×** frees a lot of memory; **Open Settings › Picture** for fewer reflections or a lower detail level.
- **VPX couldn't find the screen:** a screen the settings name wasn't there, and VPX used another. Where the settings named it the way older VPX builds did, the app names it again as your VPX needs, and it's right next game. Otherwise, check the screen is switched on: **Open Settings › Screens**.
- **Its script stopped with an error:** its line, and VPX's words for the error. Where that line sets a ball's ID, which VPX 10.8.1 refuses, it says so. Where VPX 10.8.0 is in your library, **Play it with VPX 10.8.0**; **Report it** opens Report a problem for the table.
- **VPX tried to play it in VR, and couldn't:** VPX's Enable VR setting is on, or on Autodetect with a VR program on the PC (SteamVR, say), so VPX started the table for a VR headset; that didn't work, and the table never played. Not playing in VR? **Turn VR off** sets Enable VR to Disabled in VPX's settings, and tables play on your screens from the next game. The setting itself is on Settings › Advanced.

**Dismiss** clears them. The achievements a game earned show in a notice. If VPX itself stopped with a problem, a notice says so, with **Report it**: its log goes in the report.

## Screenshots

**F9** saves each of VPX's windows as it shows (the playfield, the backglass, the score display and the topper) into the app's data folder's `Screenshots`, by table. They show on the table's page, in a **Screenshots** tile among its pictures and videos. Settings › Controls › Screenshots chooses the key: F1, F2, F4 to F9, or none (VPX's own keys aren't offered). A table can also take them by itself when a game makes its high scores: **Screenshot at a high score** in its settings.

Screenshots are taken by the app's add-on in VPX, so they need VPX 10.8.1 and a game started with PLAY.
