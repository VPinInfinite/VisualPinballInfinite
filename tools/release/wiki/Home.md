# Visual Pinball Infinite

Visual Pinball Infinite is a free, open-source pinball player and table manager for Windows 10 and 11. It plays Visual Pinball X tables (`.vpx`) on its own engine, with VPX's own physics rewritten in Rust, so tables play as their authors tuned them. Around the engine, one app looks after your library: adding tables and everything that comes with them, their pictures and videos, high scores, your screens, sound and controls, and every setting.

You don't need VPX, PinMAME or anything else installed: everything comes in one installer. You bring the tables and ROMs.
