# Your library

The library shows every table in your tables folder: its picture, maker, year and what it holds. Its pictures and facts come from the Virtual Pinball Spreadsheet (VPS), from PinUP Popper's media, or from the app itself. **Library**, **VPS browser**, **Tools**, **Table editor** and **Player hub** at the top switch between them.

## Tiles or a list

- **Tiles** show a picture of each table: its playfield, backglass or wheel (**Pictures**), at the size you set (**Tile size**).
- **The list** shows a row for each table: maker, year, rating, version, authors, what it holds (backglass, ROM, PinUP pack, AltSound pack, color DMD, sound) and when you played it. **Columns** chooses which columns show, each kind of media among them.
- **Sort** by name, year, maker, recently added or rating.
- A triangle on a tile means the table needs a look: see [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting).

Settings › Library sets what the library opens as, what tiles show and their size.

## Groups down the side

- **All tables**, **Favourites**, **Top rated** (the tables you've given stars, the most stars first), **Recently played**, **Recently added**, and **Needs attention** (the tables needing a look, by kind).
- **Smart playlists:** VPin Workshop's tables, Originals, The 1990s, and any of VPS's tags you tick in Settings › Library › Playlists (a release's features such as SSF, VR or FastFlips, or a machine's themes).
- **Your playlists:** **New playlist** makes one; **Add to a playlist…**, in a table's right-click menu or on its page, puts a table in it. Settings › Library › Playlists renames or deletes them (their tables stay in the library).
- **Browse by** maker, decade or type.
- **Has:** only the tables with a backglass, ROM, PinUP pack, AltSound pack or color DMD, or with a playfield picture or a wheel.
- **Hidden:** the tables you've hidden, when Settings › Library › Playlists shows the Hidden playlist.

## Ratings

Give a table one to five stars on its page: see [A table's page](Table-page#its-facts). Ratings you gave tables in PinUP Popper (VPin Studio keeps its ratings there too), PinballX or PinballY show until you rate a table here yourself. VPX keeps no ratings.

The first time you rate a table, where one of those front ends is found, the app asks **Put your ratings in PinUP Popper too?** (naming the ones it found):

- **Yes, put them there too:** the stars you give here show there as well, and a rating you change there shows here.
- **No, keep them here:** they stay in this app.

Settings › Library › Ratings changes it whenever you like (**Put my ratings in PinUP Popper's list too**). With VPIDB connected, **Share my ratings and which tables I have** sends them there: see [Achievements](Achievements#sharing-on-vpidb).

## Search

The search at the top finds tables by name, maker, author, ROM or file name.

## A tile's right-click

**Open its page**, **Hide** it (it then shows only in the Hidden playlist) or **Unhide** it, or **Remove from library…**

## Matching tables to VPS

Each table is matched to its entry on VPS by its file's name, the name, version and authors inside the table, and its ROM. That gives it its picture, facts and authors. When the match is only a guess, its page asks **Is this …?** Answer **Yes, that's it**, or **Not this one** and pick it yourself: search VPS by a machine's name, maker, year or ROM, then choose which of its releases you have. There are two ways to keep your choice:

- **Associate in VPI only:** the app remembers it, and nothing is renamed.
- **Overwrite for all players (VPX, PinMAME, VPin Studio):** the table's files are renamed to VPS's name for it, `Name (Maker Year)`, so other programs match it the same way, and PinUP Popper's game list is changed with them (a copy of the list is kept first). Its settings can put the names back.

**Edit its details** on a table's page sets your own name, maker, year, type, version or authors over VPS's. They're kept in the app only: the table's file and other programs keep theirs.

## The VPS browser

**VPS browser** at the top shows every machine VPS lists, as cards. Filter them by maker, era, machine type and table format, show **Only machines I don't have**, and sort by newest release, name, year or most releases. Choose what VPS should have for them, a backglass or a PinUP pack say, with its chips. A machine's card opens a panel beside the cards: who designed it, every release and file VPS links to, and **Open in my library** for one you have.

## Tools

**Tools** at the top looks after every table at once, each page with a search box: **ROMs** (see [ROM machines](ROM-machines)), **PinUP packs** (see [PinUP packs](PinUP-packs)), **Media** (see [Media](Media)), **Color DMD** (see [Score displays](Score-displays#color-dmds)) and **AltSound** (see [AltSound](AltSound)). A table's own page does the same for that table.

## The table editor

**Table editor** at the top: tables are still made in VPX's editor and played here as it makes them, and the app's own editor grows in stages. Its first two are here: the **table inspector** and the **achievements editor**. **Find a table to open** (or **Another table** once one is open), then:

- **Its parts:** the table seen from above, with every part listed by kind beside it (**Find a part by name**). The playfield shows **Its look**, as the player draws the table from straight above (**Draw its look** makes that picture the first time), or its parts' **Outlines** alone. Right-click a part to hide it, to reach the parts under it.
- **Its achievements:** the achievements editor, which makes the table's achievement packs: see [Achievements](Achievements#making-a-pack).

Click a part, on the playfield or in the list, to see:

- what it is, whether it's seen or hidden, and its layer;
- what an achievement can count of it (each hit, a spinner's spins, a slingshot's kicks), with the line a pack names it by (**In a pack**);
- every setting the table file keeps for it, read only for now.

A table's **…** menu › **Open in the table inspector** opens it there too. The table file itself never changes.

## The Player hub

**Player hub** at the top:

- **High scores:** every table's best, as its machine's memory or its script keeps it (ours or VPX's, whichever is higher), beside your own best from the games you played here, and what keeps it. A table's page has its whole list.
- **Achievements:** your packs, each opening to show what you've earned of it, and how many you've earned in **Hardcore** and in **Softcore** (click one to count only those). **Add a pack…** adds one, and a pack's bin deletes it for good. See [Achievements](Achievements).
- **Stats:** how many tables you have and have played, time played, your most played tables, your latest plays, time played each week for the last twelve weeks, and your library by decade and by maker.

## A folder for each game

Settings › Library › **Where tables keep their files** chooses between VPX's **shared folders** (every table together, every ROM in one ROMs folder, every PinUP pack in one PinUP folder) and **a folder for each game**, as Visual Pinball X 10.8.1 keeps them: each game's tables in a folder of their own with their ROM, packs, coloring and music, easy to copy, back up or delete. Switching shows what would move first (**See each game's folder**), then **Move them** moves the files, showing how far it's got, and PinUP Popper's game list follows them. **Put them back** undoes it the same way. Nothing is deleted.

Only VPX 10.8.1 and later read a folder for each game. VPX 10.8.0, and PinUP Popper or any program that starts tables through VPX 10.8.0, can't start ROM tables kept this way, nor find their PinUP packs, AltSound packs or colorings. Visual Pinball Infinite and VPin Studio read both.

## Removing a table

Right-click its tile, or use **Remove from library…** in its page's **…** menu. The window lists every file that goes with it, each with a tick box:

- Anything another table also uses starts unticked, with which table uses it.
- Its high scores, play log and screenshots start unticked too. Ticked, those are forgotten for good.
- Everything else ticked goes to the Recycle Bin, where you can get it back.

PinUP Popper's game for it comes off Popper's list too, a copy of the list kept first.
